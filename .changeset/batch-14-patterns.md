300 new designs, taking the catalog from 338 to 638. They come in twelve families drawn from subjects rather than one shape: woven and printed cloth (Tartan, Glen Check, Argyll, Fair Isle), Japanese and folk ornament (Wave Fans, Hemp Leaf, Greek Key, Talavera), op art (Twin Spiral, Square Tunnel, Cafe Wall, Zebra Wave), fields steered by noise (Jet Stream, Murmuration, Hypsometric, Camouflage), the natural world (Ginkgo, Monstera, Peacock, Woodpile), floors, walls and roofs (Flemish Bond, Crazy Paving, Rose Window, Skyline), pop from the 1950s to the 1990s (Starburst, Supergraphic, Memphis Mix, Pixel Critters), the sky (Northern Lights, Orrery, Star Trails, Lunation), things on a table (Citrus Wheel, Peppermint, Allsorts, Dice), tilings (Cairo, Knotwork, Apollonian, Boogie Woogie), print, signal and data (Ordered Dither, Signal Flags, Radar Sweep, Streamgraph) and papercraft and patchwork (Barn Raising, Cathedral Window, Paper Dolls, Rosette).

Many read where a cell sits in the sheet: a stripe that keeps its color down a column, a field of strokes that flows because neighboring cells share css-doodle's 2D noise, a spiral or a tunnel centered on the sheet. Every one exports as native SVG with no caveat, carries the catalog's tags, mood, density and uses, and has a preview image. `tabbied-mcp`'s README counts the larger catalog.
